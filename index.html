    About Us

    A magazine is an experiment and it represents a new focus, a new ratio between commerce and intellect.

    Pictoreal is one of the non-technical clubs in PICT. At Pictoreal, we create and publish an annual magazine of PICT with unique themes every year. Pictoreal organizes several enthusiastic events throughout the year to boost creative minds. The club always focuses on promoting one’s skills in literature, design, and photography. The club always supports individuals to build practical skills like event management, public oration, teamwork, and much more. To enhance one's productive skills, we organize multiple events like Pics-o-Reel, an annual art and photography exhibition-cum-competition where students can showcase their art pieces, and Manthan, an event to promote public speaking skills which consists of mini-events like extempore, debates, group discussions, etc. Pictosocial, a subgroup of Pictoreal believes the culture in PICT to lose ourselves in the service of others until we discover our own lives and our happiness. To promote the same, we organize events like Tree plantation drives, blood donation and Monetary donation drives, and Old-age home or Orphanage visits. To guide and communicate with students in remote areas, we organize Career counselling sessions. Here at Pictoreal, we try to step forward, reach out and help the students in PICT unleash their creative side.

    Our members in Pictoreal always live by one motto-

    ‘May Thoughts, Colours, and Words prevail!’

    OUR FLAGSHIP EVENTS

    • Career Guidance

      View Photos

      Career Guidance

      The career guidance activity was conducted at Pune Municipality's Madhyamik Vidyalaya, Katraj, Pune on March 15th, 2024. The purpose of the visit was to provide information to the students of 8th and 9th grades regarding career pathways after completing the 10th grade. The session encouraged them to think about their dreams and different career paths and guided them about the steps they need to take to achieve their goals. As part of an engaging activity, we encouraged students to articulate their aspirations by writing them on paper.

    • Manthan

      View Photos

      Manthan

      MANTHAN public speaking competition was held on September 14th, 2023.The competition provided a platform for students to showcase their oratory skills and express their views on the topic "Is brain drain a threat to innovation and development in home countries?". Through two rounds of extempore storytelling and debate, participants gained experience in public speaking, formulating arguments, and rebutting opposing views. The event allowed students to learn and improve their communication abilities while exploring an important socio-economic issue. Ultimately, Siddhant Vishnu, a first-year student, emerged as the winner, receiving an Amazon gift card along with the second and third-place winners. MANTHAN successfully achieved its objective of providing an opportunity for students to test and enhance their public speaking proficiency.

    • Pictofest

      View Photos

      Pics-O-Reel

      Pics-O-Reel, an art and photography exhibition-cum-competition, was organized to provide the students of PICT with a chance to showcase their artistic talents and celebrate their creativity. The event took place on 17th February 2023, and it received a staggering total of 360+ entries this year! The results were announced on 25th February, with the winners determined by tallying the votes for all the submissions. The entries that received the highest number of votes were declared the winners.

    • Blood Donation

      View Photos

      Blood Donation

      On January 31st, 2024, Pictoreal in collaboration with NSS hosted a successful Blood Donation Drive in partnership with Janakalyan Blood Bank. Dr. Kulkarni inaugurated the event, stressing community involvement and blood donation's lifesaving importance. A campus street play raised awareness, leading to over 231 donors. Post-donation, refreshments and a photo booth were provided. With support from 50+ volunteers, the event ran smoothly, managing donor flow and aiding blood bank staff. A closing ceremony expressed gratitude, celebrating the event's surpassing success and highlighting the impact of community collaboration.

    • Donation Drive

      View Photos

      Donation Drive

      Pictoreal organized its annual Donation Drive for Vol '24, reflecting its commitment to serving the underprivileged. The drive received generous contributions from students and staff, including monetary donations, clothes, footwear, bags, and books. A closing ceremony was held to distribute the donations among three beneficiary NGOs, amplifying the impact of Pictoreal's generosity.

    • BE Photoshoot

      View Photos

      BE Photoshoot

      BE photoshoot, the annual event of the college magazine club, Pictoreal was conducted on 13th April 2024 in front of the A1 Building and the Lawn. This event enabled BEs to capture and relish their final moments at the college through photography sessions. During the event, the photography volunteers captured class photos of the BE students and photographs of staff, security, library, accounts, and sports department along with the Director, Dr. P.T. Kulkarni, and the Principal, Dr. S.T. Gandhe. Alongside the photography sessions, the event had interactive activities such as the "Whisper Challenge" game, providing BE students a platform to bond and enjoy themselves. The event concluded with a short ramp walk segment where BEs showcased their sarees and suits, adding style and flair.

    • Parichay

      View Photos

      Parichay

      On October 16th, 2023, the FE introductory event, Parichay was organized to introduce the structure and workings of our club to the recently joined First Year students of PICT. It was attended by 160+ students. The heads of respective teams within Pictoreal introduced their teams to the audience. It was accompanied by various interactive and fun games related to specific teams. The PictoSocial team performed a skit, touching upon the topic of the ‘Blood Donation Drive’ with subtle humour. The event concluded with an open mic show, wherein the audience and the team members expressed their talents through music and dance.

    • Pictosocial Visit

      View Photos

      Ayodha Charitable Trust Visit

      The visit to Janseva Orphanage on November 5th, 2023, saw the enthusiastic participation of 40 dedicated volunteers. Our goal was not just to bring smiles to the children's faces, but also to ignite their creativity, promote teamwork, and instill an appreciation for cultural traditions. Through engaging activities like diya painting, killa making, and lantern crafting, we created a memorable experience for both volunteers and children alike, fostering a sense of joy and community spirit.
